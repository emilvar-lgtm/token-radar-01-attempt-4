The model, in full

Everything below follows from the snapshot's own figures — TVL, 7-day change, age with no token, chain count and category. No protocol name is ever special-cased.

Five risk factors, 0–100 each

  • Volatility — |7-day TVL change| over 60%. A swing either way is risk. No change reported: 55.
  • Youth — under 90 days: 85; under 180: 70; under 365: 55; under 730: 35; older: 20. Unknown age: 70.
  • Concentration — 1 chain: 80; 2: 60; 3: 45; 4–5: 30; 6 or more: 15.
  • Category — Bridge 85, Derivatives 75, Liquid Staking / Restaking / Prediction 70, RWA 65, Yield 60, Lending / CDP 55, Dexs 50, Insurance 45, Stablecoins 40, other 60.
  • Size — under $10M TVL: 85; under $25M: 70; under $50M: 55; under $100M: 40; under $500M: 25; under $2B: 15; larger: 10.

risk = 0.25·volatility + 0.25·youth + 0.20·concentration + 0.15·category + 0.15·size

implied = TVL × 0.30 × growth × maturity × (1 − risk ÷ 100 × 0.55)

growth = 1 + clamp(7-day change, −40%, +120%) ÷ 100 × 0.5. maturity = 0.65 + min(age, 1095 days) ÷ 1095 × 0.35, unknown age counted as 365 days. Bands: Low under 30, Moderate 30–49, Elevated 50–69, High 70 and over. The board covers all 40 watchlist protocols plus every other rankable pre-token protocol below the $50M floor — 198 rows on this snapshot.

Implied value is this entry's own claim about what a token would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is. It does not predict whether or when a token is issued.
